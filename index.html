Why forests matter

Wonder is the first step. Care is the next.

Forests hold carbon, regulate water, create rainfall and shelter most terrestrial biodiversity. They are also home to people whose knowledge and rights are central to conservation.

About the project

A digital forest encyclopedia, built one connection at a time.

Forest Explorer is a page-first atlas of 120 forests, designed to be read like a field book rather than an endless feed. Move between complete pages for forests, wildlife, maps, discoveries and stories.

Each forest profile loads a gallery of ten or more high-resolution images from the Wikimedia Commons API in the browser. The original files remain available at their source, alongside credits and licensing information.

Built for scale

The content model separates forests, species, observations, media, stories and sources so the collection can expand without losing context.

Transparent sources

Image credits, licences and verification notes travel with the content. Folklore is never presented as verified science.
